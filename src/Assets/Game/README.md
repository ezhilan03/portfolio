# Character art

`characters.png` is an AI-generated illustration made for this portfolio. The three characters are fictional representations of career chapters, rather than literal portraits. The sprite atlas is presented unchanged through three CSS background positions.

Direction: three equally sized vertical panels, dark forest-green background, detailed pixel-art South Asian fantasy characters growing visibly older from left to right. Young cloud ranger with scroll and bow; scholarly researcher with glasses and book; mature data architect with beard and staff. Muted teal, warm gold and parchment accents. No text or logos.

The landscape, quest icons and boss are scalable code-native pixel graphics in `PixelArt.js`.
